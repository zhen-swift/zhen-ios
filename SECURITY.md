# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Open a **private** security advisory on the repository rather than a public issue. If
that is not available to you, open an issue titled "security contact request" with no
detail in it and wait to be contacted.

Please include what you did, what happened, and what you expected. There is no bounty.
There is a commitment to reply, to say plainly whether something is a bug, and to credit
you unless you would rather not be.

## Use it on networks you are responsible for

This is a network scanner. It sends ICMP echo requests to every address on the network
the iPad is on, asks Bonjour and NetBIOS for names, and — when you ask — opens TCP
connections and sends UDP probes to the ports you choose on the hosts you choose.

That is unremarkable traffic on a network you run and can be unwelcome on one you do not.
Scanning a network without the owner's permission is, depending on where you are,
somewhere between rude and illegal. Scan your own network, or a client's with their
written agreement.

## What it keeps

History stays in the app's own container on the iPad: no account, no server, no
analytics. Deleting the app deletes it.
